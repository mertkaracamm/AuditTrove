AuditTrove Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Where this policy applies

AuditTrove is available in two ways: as a mobile app (iOS and Android) and as an app inside ChatGPT (the "ChatGPT App"), where the AuditTrove tool audit_document reviews documents you provide in a ChatGPT conversation. This policy covers both. Where a practice applies to only one of them, this policy says so.

We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data. In the ChatGPT App we collect no device identifiers, no usage counters, and no subscription data — only the document content, file name, and review preferences that ChatGPT sends to the audit_document tool when you ask for a review.

What we do with your documents

When you submit a document, the text extracted from it is sent to third-party AI providers solely to generate your review. Analysis is performed by OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C.); to improve accuracy, findings may additionally be cross-checked by Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC) and Google (Google LLC), which receive the same extracted text for that purpose only. The extracted text is used only to produce your review and for no other purpose. Under these providers' API data usage policies, content sent through their APIs is not used to train their models.

In the mobile app, this transfer happens only after you have given your explicit consent; consent is requested before every review, and nothing is sent if you decline. In the ChatGPT App, submitting a document to AuditTrove and asking for a review is your instruction to process it; if you do not want a document processed, do not submit it to the tool.

Documents are processed only to produce your review and are not stored on our servers. They are held in memory during analysis and discarded afterward. In the mobile app, the resulting review is saved only in your review history on your own device. In the ChatGPT App, the review is returned to your ChatGPT conversation and is retained there under OpenAI's own privacy policy; we keep no copy.

Recipients of your data

  • AI providers: OpenAI performs the analysis; Anthropic and Google may cross-check findings. Each receives only the extracted document text and review preferences, solely to generate your review, and processes it under its own privacy policy and API data usage terms, which include not using API content to train models.
  • RevenueCat (mobile app only): validates subscriptions and purchases together with your app store.
  • Expo (mobile app only): delivers the push notification that tells you a review is ready, using an anonymous device token.
  • Railway: hosts our servers. Document text passes through this infrastructure in memory during analysis but is not stored there.

We share data with no other recipients.

Retention

  • Documents and extracted text: held in memory only for the duration of the analysis, then discarded. Never written to server storage.
  • Review output: not stored on our servers. Mobile app: kept only in the review history on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. ChatGPT App: kept in your ChatGPT conversation under OpenAI's policies.
  • Anonymous usage counters (mobile app only): kept for as long as needed to enforce monthly limits, and deleted on request.
  • Subscription status and push tokens (mobile app only): kept while the app is registered with our service.

Your choices, revoking consent, and deletion

In the mobile app, consent to send a document for analysis is requested each time, immediately before the review starts, and applies only to that single review. You can withdraw consent at any moment by tapping Cancel in the consent dialog or simply not submitting a document; no standing consent is stored, so there is nothing further to revoke. In the ChatGPT App, you control what is processed by choosing what you submit; nothing is processed unless you ask for a review.

Documents are not retained on our servers, so there is no server-side copy of your documents to delete. Your review history in the mobile app exists only on your device and can be deleted by clearing it in the app or uninstalling the app. Reviews returned in ChatGPT can be managed through ChatGPT's own data controls. To request deletion of the anonymous per-device usage counters we hold, contact us at support@audittrove.com.

Children

AuditTrove is not directed to children.
