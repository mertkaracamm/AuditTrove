AuditTrove Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

What we do with your documents

When you submit a document, the text extracted from it is sent to OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C.), a third-party AI service, solely to generate your review. This transfer happens only after you have given your explicit consent in the app; consent is requested before every review, and nothing is sent if you decline. The extracted text is used only to produce your review and for no other purpose.

The documents you upload are processed only to produce your review and are not permanently stored on our servers. They are held in memory during analysis and discarded afterward; the resulting review is saved only in your review history on your own device. Under OpenAI's API data usage policy, content sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models.

Information we collect

We collect anonymous, per-device usage counts (for example, the number of reviews) to enforce free-tier limits and operate the service, and your subscription status, verified through our payments provider (RevenueCat), to unlock paid features. We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Third-party services

Document analysis is performed by OpenAI, a third-party AI service, which receives the text extracted from your document solely to generate your review. OpenAI processes this data under its own privacy policy and API data usage policy, which provide protections equivalent to those described here, including not using API content to train its models. Subscriptions and purchase validation are handled by RevenueCat and your app store. Push notifications letting you know a review is ready are delivered via the Expo push service using an anonymous device token.

Your choices, revoking consent, and deletion

Consent to send a document for analysis is requested each time, immediately before the review starts, and applies only to that single review. You can withdraw consent at any moment by tapping Cancel in the consent dialog or simply not submitting a document; no standing consent is stored, so there is nothing further to revoke.

Documents are not retained on our servers, so there is no server-side copy of your documents to delete. Your review history exists only on your device and can be deleted by clearing it in the app or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of the anonymous per-device usage counters we hold, contact us at support@audittrove.com.

Your history

Your review history is stored locally on your device. Clearing it in the app or uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

AuditTrove is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy by publishing a revised version with a new effective date.
