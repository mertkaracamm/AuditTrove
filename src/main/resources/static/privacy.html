AuditTrove Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

AuditTrove is operated by Mert Karacam. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the financial document audit service.

Data processed

When you request an audit, AuditTrove receives the PDF, its filename, and the instructions needed to perform the request. Text is extracted from the PDF and analyzed against a regulatory reference corpus.

AI processing and service providers

Extracted document text and relevant regulatory context are sent to the OpenAI API to generate the audit. The service is hosted on Railway. These providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and retention

AuditTrove does not intentionally save uploaded PDFs or extracted document text to its application database. Data is processed for the request in memory. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs or request metadata under their own policies.

Security and sensitive information

Do not upload documents containing information you are not authorized to share. Avoid unnecessary personal data, account credentials, payment card data, or other highly sensitive information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may stop processing by not submitting a document. For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning data controlled by AuditTrove, use the support channel below. Requests involving provider-controlled records may be subject to the provider's policies.

Contact

Open a support request at GitHub Issues.
