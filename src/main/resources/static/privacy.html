AuditTrove Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

What we do with your documents

The documents you upload are processed only to produce your review and are not permanently stored on our servers. They are held in memory during analysis and discarded afterward; the resulting review is saved only in your review history on your own device.

Information we collect

We collect anonymous, per-device usage counts (for example, the number of reviews) to enforce free-tier limits and operate the service, and your subscription status, verified through our payments provider (RevenueCat), to unlock paid features. We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Third-party services

Document analysis is performed using a third-party AI processing provider solely to generate your review. Subscriptions and purchase validation are handled by RevenueCat and your app store. Push notifications letting you know a review is ready are delivered via the Expo push service using an anonymous device token.

Your history

Your review history is stored locally on your device. Clearing it in the app or uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

AuditTrove is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy by publishing a revised version with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: support@audittrove.com.
