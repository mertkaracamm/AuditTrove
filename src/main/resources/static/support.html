AuditTrove Support

What is AuditTrove?

AuditTrove reviews a PDF document you upload — a lease, a subscription or service commitment, an insurance policy, a vehicle sale agreement, an employment contract, a financial report or another text-based document — and returns a structured, page-referenced review: a score, a summary, attention points, key facts, suggested actions and questions to ask before you decide.

How do I run a review?

Choose the document type, then pick a PDF, scan pages with your camera, or add photos from your gallery. Large documents are processed in the background — you can close the app, and you'll get a notification when the review is ready.

Is my document stored?

No. Documents are processed only to produce the review and are not permanently stored on our servers. Your review history stays on your device.

Subscriptions

AuditTrove Pro unlocks unlimited reviews. The free tier includes a limited number of reviews per month. Manage or cancel your subscription in your app store settings; use "Restore purchases" in Settings if a purchase doesn't appear.

Important

AuditTrove is a decision-support tool, not financial, accounting, investment, tax, or legal advice, and it does not determine whether a document is lawful or compliant. Review findings with a qualified professional before relying on them.
