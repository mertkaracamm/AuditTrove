AI-assisted document review

Before you decide, catch what you would have missed.

Upload a contract, policy, financial report or any other document. AuditTrove reads it end to end and gives you a structured review: a score, a summary and page-referenced points to watch.

What it does Supported documents
Reads all of it. No half of a long document skipped. Page-referenced. Every point is tied to its page. Consistent. Same document, same score, every time.

A review in three steps

Bring your document, let it be reviewed in the background, get your page-referenced report.

01

Bring your document

Pick a PDF, scan with the camera or add photos from the gallery. You choose the document type, the app handles the rest.

02

It reads all of it

The review runs in the background and keeps going even if you close the app. You get a notification when it is ready.

03

Get your report

A score, an executive summary, points to watch and questions for your advisor. Every point referenced to its page.

The work starts when the report lands

You do not just read a finding: you see it on the document, you ask about it, you compare two versions.

On the document

Findings on the document's own pages

Every finding is highlighted on the line it came from. Tap it, read the card, jump to the next one. No hunting for page numbers.

  • Works on scanned documents and photos too
  • Every finding is tied to a sentence that appears verbatim in the document
Same document, same score

The score follows fixed rules

The score is not left to a model's mood. Review the same document a second time and you get the same result.

  • Executive summary, points to watch and key metrics
  • Ready-made questions for your advisor
  • No amount that is not in the document can get into the report
Ask the report

Ask the document what is on your mind

The answer relies only on the document and points to the page it came from. If it is not in there, it says so instead of making it up.

  • A page badge next to every answer
  • Questions and answers are not kept on the server
Version comparison

See what changed, clause by clause

Got a new version? Clauses are matched and every changed amount and wording is gathered into one list.

  • Every change comes tagged favorable or unfavorable
  • Added and removed clauses are listed separately
  • The place of each change is marked in both documents

For any document

Before you sign or decide, know what you are agreeing to.

Your documents stay yours

Your documents are processed only to produce the review report and are not permanently stored on our servers. The result is saved only in the review history on your own device.

AuditTrove is a decision-support tool; it is not financial, accounting, investment, tax or legal advice. Findings and references must be verified by a qualified person before they are relied upon.
